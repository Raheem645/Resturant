Our story

We opened Ember & Olive with one rule: the fire does the work. Our grill burns hardwood charcoal from open until close, and every dish is built around what it does to meat, vegetables and bread.

Lamb is marinated for 24 hours. Olive oil is pressed by a family farm we visit each autumn. Nothing on the menu is frozen, and nothing is rushed.

  • 24 hrs marinade on every cut
  • 100% charcoal, no gas
  • Daily bread from the clay oven
